Diabetes Emergency Support

24/7 Diabetes Emergency Helpline

Immediate assistance for severe hypoglycemia or hyperglycemia from our expert team.

Call Now: 1-800-555-1234

Symptoms: Shakiness, sweating, dizziness, confusion, rapid heartbeat

Immediate Action: Consume 15g fast-acting carbs (glucose tablets, juice) and recheck in 15 minutes

Symptoms: Extreme thirst, frequent urination, fatigue, blurred vision

Immediate Action: Check blood sugar, take insulin as prescribed, drink water, seek medical help if symptoms worsen

  • Blood sugar remains below 70 mg/dL after treatment
  • Blood sugar above 300 mg/dL with symptoms of DKA
  • Loss of consciousness or seizures
  • Difficulty breathing or severe dehydration
