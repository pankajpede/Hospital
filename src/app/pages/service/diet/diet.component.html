Nutrition for Diabetes Management

Eating the right foods is one of the most important aspects of diabetes management. A healthy diabetes diet can help you control your blood sugar, manage your weight, and reduce risk factors for heart disease.

Our registered dietitians specialize in creating personalized meal plans that fit your lifestyle, preferences, and health goals while keeping your blood sugar stable.

There's no one-size-fits-all diabetes diet. We'll help you find what works best for you.

The Diabetes Plate Method

A simple way to plan balanced, diabetes-friendly meals without counting carbs:

Half Plate Non-Starchy Vegetables

Spinach, broccoli, carrots, peppers, tomatoes, etc.

Quarter Plate Lean Protein

Chicken, fish, eggs, tofu, beans, or low-fat dairy

Quarter Plate Whole Grains/Starchy Foods

Brown rice, quinoa, whole wheat bread, sweet potato

Foods to Include

  • High-fiber foods (vegetables, fruits, whole grains)
  • Lean proteins (fish, poultry, legumes)
  • Healthy fats (avocados, nuts, olive oil)
  • Low-fat dairy products

Foods to Limit

  • Sugary beverages and sweets
  • Processed carbohydrates (white bread, white rice)
  • Trans fats (fried foods, packaged snacks)
  • High-sodium foods

Sample Meal Plans

Breakfast

Greek yogurt with berries and nuts, or scrambled eggs with spinach and whole grain toast.

Lunch

Grilled chicken salad with mixed greens, vegetables, and olive oil dressing, or turkey wrap with whole wheat tortilla.

Dinner

Baked salmon with roasted Brussels sprouts and quinoa, or stir-fried tofu with mixed vegetables and brown rice.
